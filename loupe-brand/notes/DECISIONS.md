## 1. Brand kit §09 messaging — RESOLVED, kit updated to v1.1

`HANDOFF.md` flagged that §09's proof promise ("Every claim carries its receipt") and its
*Prefer* line ("Links every answer to the exact source") were superseded by research finding
F1: the citation layer went free across the category between Nov 2025 and Feb 2026, so leading
with provenance argues about a free feature.

**Neil ruled: take the grounding direction and replace superseded taglines.** Applied to
`brand/brandkit.html`:

| Slot | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| One sentence | Loupe turns raw case evidence into connected, source-linked intelligence. | Loupe answers questions across the whole case at once, not one document at a time. |
| Proof promise | Every claim carries its receipt. | One question, the whole case — and every answer carries the artifact it came from. |
| Prefer | "Links every answer to the exact source." | "One question, put to the whole case." + "Grounded in a model, not retrieved from a pile." |
| Avoid | — | added: "Cited answers." — true, and now free across the category; it qualifies us, it does not differentiate us. |

The *Differentiator* line ("The model is a component. The evidence layer is the product.")
was already aligned and is unchanged.

**The governing idea:** provenance is a qualifying condition, not the claim. The claim is that
a question is put to the *whole case at once* and answered by traversing a model of it, rather
than by a language model reading documents one at a time. Citation falls out of the structure.
