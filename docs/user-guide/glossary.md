# Glossary

The words Loupe uses on its screens, what each one means for your investigation, and where you see it.

## Account perspective

The account from whose point of view money comes in or goes out. The same payment is money out for the payer's account and money in for the payee's account.

## Address lookup

How addresses found in the evidence get a place on the map, set in the case's settings. Automatic sends them to an outside address service, one a second, and places them as not confirmed. Only when a person clicks keeps them in the map's Needs review list until someone looks them up.

## Agrees and Differs

The results in the facts table of Compare side by side, which sets a statement beside the one it may copy. Agrees means both statements give the same value, Differs means they give different values, and Cannot compare means one side shows Not known. An unknown value is never treated as equal.

## Also elsewhere

A mark under a message in Conversations when the same words appear in messages on other phones. It says how many messages, phones and dates hold them; a message held on two phones counts once. A stock phrase, such as a greeting, is shown but counted for nothing. Turn the marks on with Mark what also appears elsewhere. Shown where the newer Corroborate is turned on.

## Audit chain

Linked records Loupe uses to check the recorded history of events for changes or gaps.

## Batch

A saved group of selected PDF readings, reviewed and imported together.

## By Claude not confirmed

A financial value that Claude saved, changed, kept as printed or entered on a person's instruction, and that no person has confirmed yet. You see it as "Saved by Claude · not confirmed", "Changed by Claude · not confirmed" or "Kept as printed by Claude · not confirmed" beside the value. Treat it as a model reading until someone checks it against the page.

## By meaning

The part of case search that finds records by what they mean as well as by their words. The heading counts the results shown against all that were found, for example "By meaning (12 of 80, best first)", so one page is never read as all of them. Each result carries a grade: Strong match, Moderate match or Weak match, set by a model with its reason shown.

## Candidate

A possible reading that waits for review or is under review. It does not count until a person accepts it.

## Capture or snapshot

A saved set of records and settings taken at one time. It does not change when the live case changes.

## Chance

The line under a candidate link on the Corroborate page that says how often the pattern happens anyway, with its count and base, for example "Payments are on 145 of 848 days in the period." It sits beside the grade and never changes it.

## Changed since cited

A payment that a finding or theory cites has changed since it was cited. The finding keeps the values it cited and shows the new values beside them, so you can decide whether the finding still holds.

## Check first

The priority of a judgement call, set from what the value could change if it is wrong. Check first can change the money itself, Check soon can change whose money it is or which account or statement it belongs to, and When convenient can change only how a payment is described. A priority is a prompt and never stops an import.

## Checking nothing is missing

The last step of reading a phone report. Loupe checks what it read against the report, record by record, before it calls the phone ready.

## Checks made

The alternative explanations Corroborate tests on every result: routine contact, a shared household, a shared workplace, a stock phrase, a busy place, the clock or time zone, a recycled number, another person of the same name, a shared device, duplicate copies and received media. Each check says Ruled out, Weighed low, Checked, nothing found, or No data to check. Weighed low means the signal is present but this explanation accounts for it, so it counts for less.

## Coincidence likely

A Leads section for patterns that the case's ordinary days explain as well as the lead does. They stay listed, folded away, so you can check what was weighed and why it came out low.

## Compare areas over time

The Map's view of the areas you drew, night by night: who was inside each area, who was in both and in what order, and everything inside an area in the chosen window. Shown where the newer Corroborate is turned on.

## Conditional

A result that depends on stated assumptions. Change the assumptions and the result can change.

## Corrected before import

A note on a statement row whose value a person corrected before the statement was imported. The corrected value comes first and is the one used; the value as read stays beside it, struck through, with the reason when one was given.

## Corroborate

Saves the items you chose as one group and weighs how they could be linked. You start it with the Corroborate button on lists, the graph and the map, and the result opens on the Corroborate page. The records show what occurs together, never why.

## Corroborate levels

The likelihood that the people or events in a Corroborate result are linked. A stated rule sets the level from the signals in the grid, never a model, and the sentence under it gives the counts behind it.

- **Strong link**: at least three independent kinds of link set the incidents apart from ordinary days, the pattern repeats, and no other explanation is left open.
- **Probable link**: two or more independent kinds of link agree, or three agree but one other explanation is still open.
- **Possible link**: one kind of link sets the incidents apart, or two kinds that were weighed low.
- **Coincidence likely**: what is present happens as often on ordinary days, an explanation accounts for it, or it only describes how the items were picked.
- **Can't tell**: too little data, or too few ordinary days to compare with. The result says what is missing.

## Counterparty

The other name or party recorded for a payment. A printed name is not proof of who that party is.

## Custody

The record of how a source was received and handled.

## Deep investigation

A switch on the assistant page. With it on, the assistant reads the case in full, works through a checklist, checks what it finds and writes a full report; it can take 15 to 40 minutes and uses several times the model cost of a normal answer. Its progress line counts checklist items closed and findings noted, and you can stop it and keep what it has written.

## Deleted items

Where everything deleted in a case waits before it is removed for good. Items stay for the number of days the dialog states, hidden from every screen, search and the assistant. Restore brings an item back as it was; Delete now removes it for good at once.

## File missing

The stored file for this evidence is gone from the server. The record and what was read from it are kept, but the file cannot be opened or processed until you upload it again.

## Finalization

The step that writes the reviewed rows of a PDF reading to the ledger and closes that reading to further additions or edits.

## Financial transaction

A row of an imported bank or card statement. On the graph, a Transaction is different: it is a payment a model read from a document, and it is not checked against a statement.

## Fits and Contradicts

The result of a claim you wrote and confirmed, checked against the phones' records. Fits means the records agree with it, Contradicts means they show something else, and Can't tell means the records cannot decide it. A contact that is not found never makes a claim Contradicts.

## Go-between

A person through whom two others are in contact, by calls, messages or a shared document, when the two have no direct contact. Graph shows go-betweens in People, go-betweens and documents.

## Graded likelihood

Loupe never says yes or no to whether things are linked; it grades how likely a link is, by a stated rule and never by a model. A grade is a lead to check, not a finding.

- **Strong, Moderate, Weak, Faint**: the strength of a candidate link on the Corroborate page and of a possible transfer pair in Follow money. Weak and faint ones are listed, not hidden.
- The newer Corroborate result uses five levels instead: see Corroborate levels.

## Grid marks

The marks in the Corroborate grid, one cell per signal and instance. A filled dot means present, an open circle means checked and absent, and a question mark means no data. No data counts neither way.

## Hash or SHA-256

A fingerprint calculated from the exact bytes of a file or record. If one byte changes, the fingerprint changes, so matching fingerprints show a file is the one received.

## Incidents and ordinary days

What a Corroborate result compares. Incidents are the instances you asked about; ordinary days are comparable instances, such as the same weekday and hours with the same people, used to see whether the pattern is unusual.

## Included

A ledger row that counts toward totals and can be traced to its source. Its proof class still decides whether verified totals include it.

## Judgement calls

Values the statement reader chose that the page does not print outright, such as a year it inferred. Look at each one on its page, then mark it Looks right or correct it; none of them stops the import.

## Keyword list

A saved list of search terms that runs on every phone in the case. Its table counts each term on each phone, split by kind of record, and a count opens those records. A list can be attached to a theory.

## Lead

A pattern across the case's records that may show people acting together, saved on the Leads page. Loupe finds some by itself (Found by Loupe), some come from a Corroborate question you asked (Asked by you), and some from the agent (Suggested by the agent). Each is graded against the case's ordinary days, with what raises and lowers it. A lead is a reading by Loupe until a person adds it to a theory as a finding.

## Ledger

The stored financial readings that the financial screens count, search and trace.

## Links to review

Links added to a finding or theory without saying how they bear on it. They stay as they are until you mark each one Supports, Contradicts or Context.

## Look further

The next questions a Corroborate result suggests, each with a count. Pressing one runs that question as a new result.

## Looks right

What you press on a judgement call after checking it on the page. It is kept with the review and recorded under your name when the statement is imported. Correct this opens the value for correction instead.

## May be a copy of

A statement in the review queue that may be a copy of one already imported. It waits for you to compare the two and keep one. Once one is set aside, Kept instead names the statement that counts.

## Merged from

The names and keys that a merged person, company or thing replaced, with who merged them and when. You see it on the entity's panel in the graph.

## Minor units

Whole units of a currency's smallest part. For GBP, 6,162 pence means GBP 61.62; read raw figures with the currency shown beside them.

## Model reading

Text or a value that a model produced, such as an explanation of how items could be linked, a meaning, or a search result's text. It is saved under the model's name and labelled as a model reading, or "read by a model, not confirmed", until a person checks it. Only then is it recorded as that person's reading.

## Money around an event

The Financial page's list of bank rows and payment alerts on the phones from each chosen event's day to a week after. It marks a payment found both in a bank row and on a phone, a payment only on a phone, and the statement pages that are missing. Money as common in other weeks is set apart as ordinary.

## Native file

Data supplied in its system's own structured format, rather than only as a PDF.

## Needs you

A review queue item that waits for a person's decision, such as a possible copy to compare or a value to enter. When someone else saved the held work, it reads Needs and their name instead.

## No link found

The result of a search that had the data it needed and found nothing between the items. It always says what was searched, and it is not proof that no link exists. On the Corroborate page, Pairs with no link found lists each such pair.

## Not examined

A section at the end of an assistant answer. It lists each tool group you switched off and each step that could not run, so a narrowed answer is never read as complete.

## Not in this pattern

People in a Corroborate result with no link to the pattern, each with what was searched for them.

## OCR

Reading characters from an image of a page. It can misread characters, so check figures against the page.

## Ordering date

The date used to put a reading in sequence. It may differ from the transaction date, for example when the statement prints a posted date or a value date.

## People in contact

A list on the graph page of pairs of people on the phones who messaged, called or emailed each other, busiest first. Point at a row to see the two people on the graph.

## Phone layers

The phone switches on phone screens and the map. Each phone is a layer you turn on or off; Only this shows one phone, and All turns every phone back on. A phone that is off is not drawn and not counted, and the line under the switches says how many are on.

## Phone lens

The Timeline's phone entries, opened with Phones beside the toolbar: messages, calls, emails, photos, locations and other kinds of entry, with Stream to read them in time order and Lanes to compare phones or people side by side.

## Phone marks and terms

The phone screens mark where each value came from and what state it is in. For example, Mapped by the system, not yet confirmed means no person has checked the reading; Machine-generated means a model produced the text; Deleted means the phone marked the record as deleted; No data means the phone holds no records for that period, which is not the same as no activity. The phone glossary has more.

## Photo data

A photo's date, place and camera as the file records them. It is labelled "From the photo's data, not checked by a person", and a camera's clock can be wrong.

## Population

The set of readings an answer is built from, such as all imported payments or verified payments only.

## Provisional

Recorded for working use while the identity or other details are still unconfirmed.

## Read again by an independent tool

A phone analysis that reads each phone again with an independent open-source tool and compares that reading with the report. Its tabs show the differences: Totals differ, Only the tool read, Only in the report, Times differ, Content differs, Read the same, and Not compared. The tool's readings stay the tool's and are never merged into the report's records.

## Read by Loupe not confirmed

A value Loupe read from a file by itself, such as a date written in a document or the map position of an address, which no person has checked. Confirm it or correct it where it is shown. A correction keeps the reading on record, and the corrected value is the one used.

## Reconciliation

Comparing a statement's recorded movements with its opening and closing balances or printed totals.

## Records missing

Gaps in the records behind a Corroborate result, such as a phone with no records for one of the nights asked about.

## Saved subsets

A selection kept under a name in Corroborate, so you can bring it back later or combine it with another one.

## Scenario

A calculation saved with the records and assumptions it used. Changing a scenario does not change the evidence.

## Set aside

Kept in the case but left out of every total, with the reason on record. You can set aside a statement row yourself; Loupe sets aside copies of statements already imported and fund statements that hold no payments. Phone lists such as monitoring apps also have items set aside by a person. Nothing set aside is deleted. A row you set aside can be let back in, with your reason on record.

## Show routes

A switch on the map that draws each phone's path in time order. Gaps of more than 2 hours are not drawn and are counted instead, and positions with no time are not part of a route.

## Source check

The line at the end of an assistant answer that counts how its citations hold up: how many support their sentence, support part of it, do not mention it, or could not be checked. It says who did the checking: code, or a named model, never a person. A sentence none of whose sources mention it is marked "(the cited source does not mention this)".

## Source reference

The file, page, cell or other place that supports a reading.

## Statement control

A printed date, balance or total used to check a reviewed statement.

## Stock phrase

Words so common in messages, such as a greeting or ok thanks, that finding them on two phones means nothing. Loupe shows them and counts them for nothing.

## Story lanes

The view titled What happened, person by person, opened from a column of the Corroborate grid. It gives one lane per person with the events numbered in time order and coloured by kind of record.

## Superseded

Replaced by a later reading, which is the one that counts. The earlier reading stays in the history.

## Supports Contradicts and Context

How an item you add bears on a theory or finding. Supports and Contradicts take a side; Context is the default and takes none.

## Tools for this question

The card on the assistant page that shows the tool groups chosen for your question and their state in this run: In use, Chosen, not used yet, Available, not chosen, Off, or Always on. Switch a group off or on and the change applies from the next step.

## Trail

Trail: how this was built, on a Corroborate result. It lists each step of the selection; a step marked added nothing did not change the result.

## Unchecked claims

Claims drawn from a file that were never checked against the file's text, so they are not in the graph. Check them reads each claim against its file and spends from the case's AI budget; claims that pass are added to the graph when the check ends.

## Verified period

A statement period whose rows add up to its printed opening and closing balances, or whose file totals were checked and agree. Its payments count in verified totals without a person confirming them.

## Verified totals

Totals that count only payments from verified periods. A period that does not add up, prints no balance or has a check that is incomplete is marked not fully checked; working totals count it and verified totals leave it out. Choose Verified payments only to see them.

## Who overlaps with whom

A Dossiers view of every pair of people in the case against every kind of overlap: contact direct or through others, groups, shared words and files, places, access logs, money and documents. A square shows the pair's level from its lead; pick one to see why. A case with more than 10,000 phone records and positions is too large for it, and the view says so.

## Workspace

The case area that keeps saved theories, findings, notes and workpapers with their attachments.
