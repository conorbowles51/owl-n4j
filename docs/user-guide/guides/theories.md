# Theories guide

A theory is an explanation of what happened, built from the case's own records. It sits in Workspace beside findings, notes and tasks. Each item added to a theory keeps why it was added, how it bears on the theory, who added it and when. A finding is a conclusion a person stands behind; a theory can become one.

## Add an item to a theory

**Add to theory…** sits on most screens: the ledger, phone records, conversations, the Graph, the Timeline, the Map, Evidence, search results, dossiers and agent answers. It takes one item, or a group of items chosen together.

The dialog **Add to a theory** says what goes in, then asks:

- **Why this, in your words**, for example "the account the money ends in" or "every payment under 100 in March".
- **How it bears on the theory**: **Supports**, **Contradicts** or **Context**. Context is chosen to start with, so one click adds the item without taking a side. Supports and Contradicts take a side.
- **What it shows (optional)**: your own account of what the item shows. Or tick the box to ask the model to write it, citing the items. A model's account is saved as a model reading, with the model's name, until you confirm or replace it.
- **Add to**: pick a theory, finding or note, or name a new theory to start one from this item.

After the add, **Undo** removes exactly what was added.

***When to use it.*** Add an item as soon as it bears on an explanation, with the reason while it is fresh. Choose Contradicts as readily as Supports: a theory that records what speaks against it can be tested honestly.

## Weigh what supports and contradicts a theory

Open the theory in Workspace. Its cited items are listed with their relationship. Open **Compare what supports and contradicts it (N for, M against)** to see the two sides next to each other, with the count of items linked as context below them. Every kind of item counts: payments, phone records, documents, graph items and casework.

Select any item to open it.

***Reading it.*** A side that reads "Nothing is marked as supporting it" or "Nothing is marked as contradicting it" means no one has classified an item that way yet. It does not mean the case holds none.

## Classify links to review

Items linked to a theory before the three choices existed have no relationship. The theory lists them under a heading such as "3 links were added without saying how they bear on this theory", with "They stay as they are until you choose."

Anyone who can edit the case sees **Supports**, **Contradicts** and **Context** beside each one. Choose one per item. Until you choose, the item stays out of both sides of the comparison.

## Changed since cited

A payment cited in a theory, finding or note keeps the values it had when it was cited: amount, direction, date, description, payee and reference. When the payment changes later, for example through a correction, the cited item is marked **Changed since cited**, in the theory and in its line in the Workspace list.

The panel **Changed since cited** says: "This theory still shows the values it cited. They are not updated here." For each changed payment it gives what changed:

- **Changed**: one or more values differ, shown as "Amount: cited 2,548.20 USD, now 2,584.20 USD".
- **Corrected by a later version**: a later reading replaced the one cited.
- **No longer counted in Transactions**: the payment was set aside.
- **No longer in this case**: the payment is gone.

**Open current payment** opens the payment as it stands now. A cited payment with no saved values to compare is counted in a line of its own.

Phone records carry their own notes on each link, under **Corrected since collected** and **Reading changed since collected**, with **Keep** or **Remove**.

***What to do with it.*** Read the change and decide whether the theory still holds with the current value. The theory keeps the cited value so that what was relied on stays visible; the current value is the one used in totals and money flows.

## Merged from

When graph entities are merged, links from theories, findings, notes and saved groups move to the merged entity. Each link keeps its reason, relationship and narratives, and notes what it pointed at before.

On the Graph, the merged entity's details show **Merged from**: the names and keys it replaced, who merged them and on which date. "Some sources could not be removed from the graph" marks a merge that did not finish.

***Reading it.*** Use it to check that a theory's item still points at the right person or company after a merge, and to see which earlier names were folded in.

## Agent answers in a theory

An agent answer added to a theory opens the agent conversation at that answer, marked, when you select it from the theory. The answer stays a model reading.

## Turn a theory into a finding

**Convert to finding** on a theory starts a finding from it. The finding carries the theory's narratives and records. Each narrative is labelled "From the theory" with the theory's title. The theory keeps its own narratives and their history.

## Add your steps to a theory

Loupe keeps a list of the steps you take in a case: each screen and view you open. The history strip at the foot of every case screen shows your last steps; select one to return to exactly that view. When the strip is folded away, press **History** to show it.

**All steps** on the strip opens **All your steps in this case**. Only you see these, and they are kept for 90 days.

1. Search your steps by name, for example "BBVA" or "Graph".
2. Tick the steps that led to a finding. Shift-click ticks every step between two.
3. Press **Add N steps to a theory…** and complete the **Add to a theory** dialog.

The theory then holds the trail of views that led to the conclusion, which anyone reading the theory can follow.

**Clear my history** removes your steps for the case. Trails already in a theory keep their steps.

## Corroborate a theory line

Where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you, a **Corroborate this claim** button sits beside theory lines. See the Corroborate guide.

## Export the casework report

On the Reports page, **Casework report** prints the case's findings, including financial findings saved from the ledger, then theories and notes, in the order you set. Untick a section to leave it out, and move sections up or down.

For each theory and finding, the report prints:

- each cited item with whether it supports or contradicts, the reason it was added, who added it and when;
- production numbers beside cited pages;
- narratives with who wrote them; model readings are marked as model readings with the model's name;
- accepted counter-arguments, with the model's text marked as the model's;
- the theory's timeline.

Choose a **Marking** and press **Export casework report (PDF)**. The marking is your choice, not a legal privilege determination. The audit log is not included.
