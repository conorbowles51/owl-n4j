## Why v4

Corpus v3 (36 PDFs, 49 distinct periods) was clean 200 dpi synthetic scans of four layout
families, so its rates are an upper bound. v4 appends 56 PDFs / 75 periods for the shapes v3
lacked. Every v3 PDF regenerates byte-identical and every v3 ground-truth record is unchanged
(`backend/tests/test_financial_benchmark_corpus.py` checks both). v4 uses new account numbers
only, so it cannot change how a v3 period is assessed.

Ground truth: `auto` = the source alone establishes every fact; `hold` = the source cannot
complete the period (a missing page); `not_statement` = a teller receipt, which must never be
admitted as a statement period. Admitting a `hold` or `not_statement`, or saving any wrong
critical field, is a wrong admission (exit 1).

## Ranked levers (recoverable v4 periods each would unlock; each period counted once)

1. **Degraded-image OCR (low dpi, faint print, JPEG): 12 periods (15 with the combined cases).**
   Andrews 150 dpi ×2, 100 dpi ×2 (not detected), faint ×2, faint + digit ×2; generic 100 dpi,
   faint, damaged digit on a poor scan; Capital One 150 dpi + JPEG. Pattern: full-page OCR or
   the c-safety crop verification yields `?` or "running balance could not be read separately",
   so the period is held. Candidates: upscale + contrast normalisation before Tesseract, and
   verification thresholds tuned on degraded crops. Safety must be re-measured on this subset.
2. **No-activity proof for recognised Mexican layouts: 11 periods.** Scotiabank 3, Santander
   investment sections 4, Kapital USD products 2, Monex 2. `statement_admission.assess_admission`
   accepts printed proof only as `printed_transaction_count == '0'` in both directions (BBVA
   prints counts; these do not) or a reader's `no_activity_evidence` (only Andrews supplies it).
   The Scotiabank catalog builds `zero_activity_evidence` that never reaches admission; Monex,
   Kapital and Santander print zero *amount* totals plus equal balances. **Needs Neil:** whether
   printed zero totals + equal balances count as source proof.
3. **Deskew before OCR row assembly: 7 periods (10 with combined).** 1° drops a row's right
   half 9 pt over a 520 pt line, more than half a line; every skewed period fails.
4. **Unsupported layouts: 3 periods.** Scotiabank with a movements table (falls to generic,
   blocked on currency), Monex with a movements page (whole contract, including its zero USD
   section, unrecognised). These layouts are invented, as no reader exists; confirm against
   real documents before building.
5. **Image reread for BBVA and Capital One damaged digits: 2 periods.** The quality-triggered
   source-image reread repairs generic/Credit One/Merrick/Andrews digits but left `12,75O.59`
   (BBVA) and `$3B.74` (Capital One) for a person.
6. **BBVA image-only full-page OCR: 1 period** (rows split/misplaced; held on balance).

Effort-only (no readiness change): route receipts out of statement batches (2 items stay as
attention items today); the 3 unmatched items above.

## Secondary findings

- Harness bug fixed: it compared the ledger's posting date with the truth transaction date;
  Capital One prints distinct Trans/Post dates and was scored as 11 wrong dates although the
  ledger was right. Now transaction date is compared and a truth `posted_date` checked separately.
- Credit One reads only Previous/New Balance as controls; its printed Payments/Purchases totals
  are not checked (found when a generator bug printed an inconsistent total and the period was
  still admitted with the correct rows).
- The text-alignment column gap is 3× the page's median word gap, so a sparse page (few
  multi-word phrases) merges label/value cells (`Numero 510…`). Real statements carry
  disclosure text, so the corpus adds it; a sparse live page could still lose its layout.
