- Preserve all source documents and investigator decisions. Never retry an entire import merely to dismiss a stale status.

## Continuous functional and usability assessment

The user requested ongoing assessment of UI, usability and functionality, not just error collection. At each upload, reading, review, correction, import, duplicate decision, investigation and return/reopen step, check:

- **Correctness:** source coverage, account/period/currency assignment, amounts, duplicate-safe totals and persistence.
- **Clarity:** does the screen explain the current file/period, what is saved versus pending, why work is blocked and what changed after the action?
- **Actionability:** can the investigator reach and correct the indicated field, see the original and return without losing their place?
- **Efficiency:** unnecessary clicks, repetitive confirmation, bulk actions, waiting, scrolling and whether completed work stays completed.
- **Recovery:** failures retain received files and edits, explain the actual problem and offer a working next step.
- **Accessibility and layout:** discoverable controls, keyboard focus, readable status, narrow layouts and navigation that remains available on long pages.
- **Investigation value:** complete-scope totals, currency separation, source-linked findings and clear distinctions between evidence and hypotheses.
