# Round R1: first real phones

**When:** Conversations, Phone summary, Needs a decision and Add to… are live on real phones. **On what:** a real phone in a case the participant already works on, read by the participant. **Pack:** this file and `common.md`.

**Setup:** the participant opens their own case and a phone of their choosing. The moderator sees only the participant's screen in the room and writes counts and the participant's words about the interface; nothing from the phone is written down. Start with one practice task on the training case (R0 T2) so the participant meets the screens on invented data first.

**What R1 decides:** the origin wording "Mapped by the system, not yet confirmed"; the thread list collapsing below 1440 px; Ctrl/Cmd+F finding in results first; Report a problem without a screenshot; labels as tagged notes; the 10-check team checklist.

## Task cards

**T1 (8 min).** "Decide whether this phone is worth your time and where you would start."
- Target: whose phone, period, top 5 people, what is missing and where to start, in 3 min or less.
- Probe P2 on a No data band.

**T2 (8 min).** "Pick someone who matters to your case. Show everything between them and the phone's owner between two dates you choose."
- Target: 45 s or less and 6 actions or fewer.
- Probe P4 on the resulting count.

**T3 (6 min).** "Go to the first message on a date you choose in that conversation, and see what else happened around that time."
- Target: 30 s or less and 4 actions or fewer to the date; context in 2 actions.
- Probe P1 on a message.

**T4 (10 min).** "Check the three readings that affect the most records on this phone."
- Target: median 20 s or less per reading after examples are opened.
- Probe P3 on a row marked as not yet confirmed.

**T5 (6 min).** "Put the messages you think matter into a finding, so a colleague can see why."
- Target: one item in 3 actions or fewer; the reply's counts match the selection.

**T6 (5 min).** "If something here was wrong, tell the team from inside Loupe."

**T7 (10 min, separate).** Hand the participant the team checklist and ask them to complete as much as they can. Record how many checks were completed and how many results were left blank.

End with the System Usability Scale.
