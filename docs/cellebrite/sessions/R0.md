# Round R0: the phone preview on invented phones

**When:** once the phone preview is live (it is). **On what:** the preview of Phone summary, Conversations, Needs a decision and Add to…, on the training case. **Pack:** this file and `common.md`.

**Setup for each participant:** open the training case, add `?phone-prototype=on` to the address, and leave the browser on the case's first page. Read the consent wording and the case story (`common.md` sections 2 and 3).

**What R0 decides** (each is a decision in 07 that this round can keep or reverse): page layout and density at the participant's own screen size; the combined people label on a row; the time strip; the From and To wording; whether **Looks right** after viewing every kind of example is understood; and whether Add to… is found without help.

## Task cards

Read each card aloud exactly. Time limits are for the moderator only.

**T1 (5 min).** "You have just been given these two phones. Find out whose phone C5 is, what period it covers, and whether anything on it needs a decision from you."
- Target: answered from the first phone page in 3 min or less.
- Probe P2 here, on a No data band if one is shown.

**T2 (5 min).** "Who does the owner of C5 talk to most? How many messages are there between them?"
- Target: name and count in 60 s or less; the count matches the conversation's own count.

**T3 (5 min).** "Read what the two owners said to each other on the busiest day of their conversation."
- Target: lands on the day in 4 actions or fewer.
- Probe P1 here, on a message.

**T4 (5 min).** "Open one message and copy its details so you could paste them into a report."
- Target: 3 actions or fewer; the pasted text names the time with its zone and the sender.

**T5 (8 min).** "The system has read some things from these phones that nobody has checked. Check one of them and record your decision."
- Target: the participant opens each kind of example before deciding; decision in 2 min or less.
- Probe P3 here, on a row marked as not yet confirmed.

**T6 (5 min).** "Put three messages between the owners into the dossier of the person you think matters most."
- Target: 6 actions or fewer; the participant can say how many were added.

**T7 (3 min).** "Something on this screen did not work the way you expected. Tell the team about it from inside Loupe."
- Target: finds Report a problem without help.

**T8 (2 min).** "Find out what the mark on this row means." (point at an origin mark)
- Target: reaches the meaning in 2 actions or fewer.
- Probe P4 at the end, on the count of messages between the two owners.

End with the System Usability Scale (`common.md` section 6).
