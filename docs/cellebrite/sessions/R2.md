# Round R2: screens, patterns and people

**When:** the phone screens and the patterns work are finished. **On what:** real phones in the participant's own case. **Pack:** this file and `common.md`.

**Setup:** as R1. One practice task on the training case first (R0 T5).

**What R2 decides:** machine text confirmed on use rather than in a queue; lens and layer defaults stored per person and case; reading positions for casual reading kept in the browser; no evidence-by-theory matrix.

## Task cards

**T1 (6 min).** "Search all the phones for a word that matters to your case and read the best hit in its conversation. Then go back to your results."
- Target: query to hit read in context and collected in 60 s or less.

**T2 (10 min).** "Some numbers on these phones may belong to the same person. Decide on the first five suggestions."
- Target: median 20 s or less per decision; the participant can say why each was suggested.
- Probe P3 on a suggested link.

**T3 (8 min).** "Where was this phone in the hour around a message you choose? Were two people in the case together that week?"
- Target: positions within 3 actions; a two-person check in 5 min or less.

**T4 (6 min).** "List every deleted message between the owner and one other person."
- Target: 3 min or less; 3 actions or fewer from the Phone summary.
- Probe P2 on a No data band.

**T5 (5 min).** "Split the review of this phone between you and a colleague, then say what is left to review."
- Target: says what remains in 30 s or less.

**T6 (4 min).** "Close Loupe. Open it again and carry on reading where you stopped."
- Target: 2 actions or fewer.

**T7 (6 min).** "Compare what two phones hold about one person."
- Target: 3 min or less.
- Probe P4 on a count shown on both phones.

End with the System Usability Scale.
