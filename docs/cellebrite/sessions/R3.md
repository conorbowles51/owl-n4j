# Round R3: phones with the ledger and the agent

**When:** the phone and ledger crossings are finished. **On what:** a case with phones and a bank statement; the training case for practice, the participant's own case for the main tasks. **Pack:** this file and `common.md`.

**Setup:** as R1. Practice task on the training case: "Did the two owners message each other on the days of the payments to the storage business?"

**What R3 decides:** whether a person reading the money lane, statement checks and the agent's answers can tell what the system worked out from what a person confirmed; whether an evidence-by-theory matrix is wanted.

## Task cards

**T1 (8 min).** "Pick a payment on the statement. What did each phone do in the two hours around it?"
- Target: 4 min or less to answer and save; the participant says the payment has a date but no time, if so.
- Probe P1 on a message in the window.

**T2 (12 min).** "Here is a short statement a witness made (moderator gives a 3-claim statement written for the participant's case). Check it against the phones."
- Target: checked and attached in 10 min or less; the participant can say which claims the phones support, undercut or cannot test.

**T3 (8 min).** "Ask Loupe's assistant who the phone's owner was in contact with around a payment you choose. Check one thing it tells you."
- Target: the participant opens the record behind the answer and says whether it is confirmed or read by the system.
- Probe P3 on a value in the answer.

**T4 (6 min).** "Explain to a colleague how one finding was found."
- Target: 5 min or less, using the finding's own explanation.
- Probe P4 on a count in the explanation.

**T5 (6 min).** "Prepare the messages between two people for counsel."
- Target: 2 min or less of active time and 8 actions or fewer; the participant notices how many rows rely on readings not yet confirmed.

End with the System Usability Scale.
