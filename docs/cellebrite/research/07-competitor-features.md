## Findings by area

1. **Conversation review.** Threaded views in AXIOM (M9) and Reader (R1). XAMN's Q4 2025 "Participant Mode"
   groups chats and calls between the same people across apps (X1), the only documented cross-app threading by
   participant. Relativity's Short Message Viewer: participant lists and filter, inline images, reactions,
   EDITED and DELETED labels, a timeline navigator, dtSearch, redaction (RL1); limits: a filtered conversation
   cannot be coded or produced, missing data can appear as "reconstructed or unknown conversations",
   recommended file size 250 MB or less (RL1); RSMF per conversation in 24-hour slices (RL3). Everlaw: no hit
   highlights or redactions in Chat view (E1). CSV export produced confusing tables for one user (F2).
   [inference: legal tools filter and produce well but get data second-hand and slice by day.]
2. **Identity.** Pathfinder Person Resolution merges numbers, user ids and usernames, with add and split and
   cross-case presence (PF1, PF2). Oxygen Aggregated Contacts and drag-to-merge in the social graph (O3). AXIOM
   Connections "wasn't built for traditional link analysis" (M1). No tool documented its number-normalisation
   rules.
3. **Timeline.** PA, AXIOM, Oxygen, XAMN, i2 (R3, M9, O1, X2, AN1). Problems: hidden defaults (set timestamp
   fields to "Show All", R3); AXIOM portable cases lose the timeline (M6); one report with UTC+0, UTC+3 and no
   zone (F1); verify against hex (P2). [inference: no tool shows source zone and conversion per event.]
4. **Maps.** PA location carver with confidence score (P4); Pathfinder "Mutual Location" and CDR maps (PF4,
   PF3); Guardian Investigate correlates UFDR and CDR locations (G2); Oxygen frequent places, shared places,
   routes (O1). Defense material: towers are directional and phones pick the strongest signal (D4); a Kentucky
   appeal challenged Cellebrite GPS reliability and the denial stood (D5).
5. **Link analysis.** i2 imports calls and transactions with counts and summed currency on one link (AN1,
   AN2); Oxygen common contacts and shortest paths (O2, O3); Pathfinder (PF4); IPED (I1); Maltego is OSINT, not
   device parsing (MT1).
6. **Media AI.** Pathfinder categories and speech-to-text (PF4); Magnet deepfake, money and nudity classifiers
   and camera-original checks (M3, M8); Oxygen faces and OCR (O1); Belkasoft classification and video
   speech-to-text (B3); IPED face similarity, nudity, Tesseract OCR, cloud transcription (I1). No independent
   accuracy figures.
7. **Search.** Reader fuzzy by default ("hank" matches "thanks"), no queries (R1), no hex search (R2);
   Pathfinder alias search (PF1); Relativity proximity, fuzzy, stemming (RL1); IPED regex with validators (I1).
8. **AI assistants.** Magnet Copilot with citations, cloud then offline, top licences only (M2, M4); BelkaGPT
   offline, citations (B1, B2); Cellebrite Guardian summarises threads including foreign-language ones (G4).
   Criticism: AI conclusions often cannot be traced (G5). [inference: citations are the minimum.]
9. **Tagging and collaboration.** Reader tags (R1, R2); AXIOM portable-case merge-back (M6); Guardian sharing
   with defense listed as recipients (G1) and task tracking (G3); Relativity and Everlaw full coding.
10. **Reporting.** Reader and PA: PDF, Word, Excel, HTML, UFDR (R2, E2); Relativity converts UFDR chats to RSMF
    and advises trimming in Reader first (RL2); Belkasoft and AXIOM export RSMF (B4); Nuix ingests RSMF 1.0 and
    2.0 (N2); Magnet Review exports exhibits (M7).
11. **Integrity.** PA strongest: source database and table per artifact, database viewer and hex, warnings
    that carving gives false positives (P1, P3). Reader shows an SMS's source file only if included (R1); AXIOM
    unlicensed reviewers cannot see hex or file system (M6). Defense sources: the UFDR is not the full
    extraction; a Reader report "can easily be redacted and must be validated with the original extraction"
    (D1, D2, D3).
12. **Multi-source and financial.** Pathfinder ingests phones, computers, CDRs, cloud (PF4, PF3); Nuix
    cross-references several images (N1); IPED scales to 135 million items (I1). **No phone-review tool found
    ingests bank statements or transactions**; only i2 handles calls and transactions together, as structured
    imports (AN1).
13. **Usability and cost.** Free: Reader, XAMN Viewer, IPED. Built for non-technical reviewers: Magnet Review,
    Guardian. AXIOM about $8,671 a year at San Diego PD (PR2), $5k to $8k forum quotes (PR1); Oxygen $7,495 first
    year and $2,900 renewal at one Minnesota city (O4). Cellebrite gets mixed interface reviews (F4); Reader slow
    over 30 GB (F3).

## The 15 most valuable features for a defense investigator with phone productions and financial records

1. Jump to source with deleted and carved status on every item (D1, D2, P3).
2. Time zone shown per timestamp, stored and converted side by side (F1, P2).
3. One timeline across phone events and financial transactions [inference].
4. Identity resolution across apps, phones and bank counterparties (PF1) [extension is inference].
5. Conversation review by participant across apps (X1, RL1).
6. Citations on every AI answer (M2, B2, G5).
7. A check of what the production is missing (D1, D3).
8. Location points with source, accuracy radius and carved status (D4, D5, P4).
9. Exact search with fuzzy optional (R1, I1).
10. Tags and notes across phone and financial items with review status (R1, M6, RL1).
11. Exhibit export with citations, RSMF where needed (RL1, B4, M7).
12. Who-contacted-whom graph with frequency and payment flows (AN2).
13. Media OCR and transcription, bringing payment screenshots and voice notes into search (O1, I1, B3).
14. Translation of foreign-language chats (G4).
15. Usable by a non-examiner at small-firm cost (R1, M6, PR2, O4).

## Where the market is weak (inference)

- Phone data and financial records together in one timeline, one set of people, one search.
- Defense-side integrity: source, deleted state and production gaps first on screen.
- Per-event stored and converted time.
- Legal review tools lose phone context through conversion.
- Traceable AI.
- Identity across data types.
- Cost and access for small firms.

Not established: Nuix's current UFDR support, XAMN Viewer's exact features, Everlaw's deleted-message
handling, independent media-AI accuracy, any tool's number-normalisation behaviour.
