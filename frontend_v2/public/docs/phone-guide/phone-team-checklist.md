# Phone team checklist

Ten short checks of the phone screens in this build. They use the training case, which holds only invented phones and people, and take about 30 minutes.

**Updated: 1 November 2026.**

For each check, write one result: **Pass**, **Fail**, **Blocked** (something stopped you before you could try it) or **Not run**. Record what actually happened; do not pass a step just because a button could be clicked. If something is wrong or confusing, use **Report a problem** on that screen and note the check number.

## 1. Open the preview

1. Open the training case. Add `?phone-prototype=on` to the end of the address and press Enter.
2. Select **Phones (preview)** in the left sidebar.

**Pass if** the Phone summary opens and its heading names a phone. **Then** add `?phone-prototype=off` to the address: the sidebar item must disappear.

## 2. Read the Phone summary

Read the line at the top of the Phone summary.

**Pass if** you can say, without opening anything else, how many records were read, the first and last dates, and how many things need a decision. Write the three answers in the note.

## 3. Find the busiest person

Find the person with the most messages on this phone.

**Pass if** the count beside the name equals the count shown when you open that person's conversation.

## 4. Read a day in a conversation

1. Open **Conversations** and pick a thread.
2. Click a day on the time strip under the messages.

**Pass if** the stream lands on that day and the day header names the day and the time zone.

## 5. Open a record and copy it

1. Select a message and press `r`.
2. Select **Copy**.
3. Paste into a text editor.

**Pass if** the pasted text holds the time with its zone, the sender and the message, and the record panel says where the record came from.

## 6. Check a reading

1. Open **Needs a decision**.
2. Try **Looks right** before opening any example.
3. Open every kind of example, then select **Looks right**.

**Pass if** **Looks right** cannot be used until every kind is shown, and afterwards the reading says it was confirmed by you with a reason.

## 7. Add messages to a dossier

1. In **Conversations**, select three messages with Space or Shift+click.
2. Choose **Add to…**, then a dossier.

**Pass if** the reply says how many were added and how many were already there, and each message then shows that it is in a dossier.

## 8. Use the keys

Press `?`.

**Pass if** a list of keys opens, and `j`, `k` and Space move and select as the list says, with the focused row clearly visible.

## 9. Open the guide and the glossary

1. Select **Guide** at the top of the phone screen. Open **Team checklist**, then **Glossary**.
2. On any mark on a row, select **Learn more**.

**Pass if** each opens, and **Learn more** lands on that mark's meaning.

## 10. Report a problem

1. Select **Report a problem**.
2. Choose what you were doing, then **Send report**.

**Pass if** Loupe confirms the report was saved and gives a reference.

## Afterwards

Send the completed table to the team. Notes should describe the screens and what you expected, never case content.
