# Corroborate guide

Corroborate weighs how the records could link items you choose anywhere in a case. The records show what occurs
together, never why, and every result lists what was searched, including where nothing was found. The newer version,
from [The selection tray](#the-selection-tray) onwards, appears only where it has been turned on for you, in place of
the classic one.

## Start a look

***Use it*** to see every way the records could tie together a few payments, passages, phone records or graph items.

1. Choose the items on any screen that has **Add to theory**: the Graph, Significant, the Timeline, Evidence, the
   ledger, the Map, dossiers, assistant answers, phone records, conversations, passages and search results.
2. Press **Corroborate** beside **Add to theory**.
3. The dialog says how many of the chosen items can be compared and names, by kind, any that cannot be compared yet.
4. Press **Start a new look**. If a look is already in progress, **Add to the look in progress** adds the new items to
   it, so a payment, a passage and a phone record from different screens form one group.

While a look is open, a note in the corner of every case screen reads **Looking into how N items relate**. **Open**
returns to the report. **Finish this look** closes it, so the next Corroborate starts a new one.

## Read the Corroborate report

The report page opens as soon as the look starts. While it works, it names the current step. You can leave the page;
the analysis carries on. If it stops, the page says why and offers **Run again**.

The report holds these parts, top to bottom:

| Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Summary line | Items, pairs, candidate links by grade, pairs with no link, and the time taken |
| The items | Each item with its kind and date, or "no date on record" |
| Candidate links | Possible links, strongest first: the grade, the kind of link, the items, the reasons and the chance |
| Pairs with no link found | Each pair where nothing was found, with what was searched and what was out of reach |
| Analyses run | Every analysis, the pairs it read and the links it found |

***Grades.*** Each candidate link is **Strong**, **Moderate**, **Weak** or **Faint**. Weak links stay on the list,
marked as weak. Kinds of link include close in time, order of events, the same number, email or name, the same amount,
the same place at the same time, a relay, both quiet at once, and alternating use.

***Chance.*** The **Chance** line says how often the link would turn up anyway, for example how many days in the period
have payments. A link that would turn up on most days carries little weight however many times it appears.

***No link found.*** A pair with no link says what was searched. It is not proof that no link exists. **Out of reach**
names a search that could not be made and why.

Long lists end in **Show more**; the counts cover every row. **Export report (PDF)** and **Run again** sit above.

## Ask how the items could be linked

**Ask how they could be linked** opens the case assistant with the question already written. The assistant's
explanations appear under **How they could be linked**, marked as a model reading with the model's name. Each states
what it predicts, what speaks for and against it with citations, the record that would tell it apart from the others,
and what was searched.

To keep an explanation, choose a theory and press **Accept as supports** or **Accept as contradicts**. The theory
records it as written by the model and accepted by you.

## The selection tray

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

Everything you choose for Corroborate joins one selection for the whole case. The tray in the corner of every case
screen lists each part as a chip with its count and the screen it came from, and the total. The selection stays after
a reload and when you move between screens, until you clear it.

- **Corroborate** opens the question menu on the whole selection. **Paste a list**, **Check a claim** and **Save or
  combine** open the tools below. The cross on a chip removes that part; **Clear** empties the selection.
- A chip that says "not readable yet" counts items Corroborate cannot weigh yet. They are named, not dropped.

With nothing selected, the tray offers **Paste a list…**, **Saved subsets…** and **Check a claim…** only.

## Ways to add items

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

| Where | What to do | What joins the selection |
|---|---|---|
| Any screen with Add to theory | Press **Corroborate** | The chosen items |
| Graph | Press **Lasso to corroborate**, then draw round the items | The items inside the shape |
| Map | Draw an area, then press **Corroborate this area** | Every phone position, photo and place inside it |
| Payments, evidence files, timeline events | Right-click and choose **Corroborate this (C)**, or point at the item and press C | That item |
| Timeline, Conversations, Search | Press **Corroborate N** | Every row of the selection, not only the rows on screen |
| Tray | **Paste a list…** of phone numbers, account numbers or plates | Every item in the case that matches |
| Evidence | **Select all N files**, then **Corroborate** | Every file of the filter, across all pages |

Each way opens the question menu once the items are in. On the Graph, if only part of the case is drawn, the note says
how many items are shown and that the lasso covers those. A pasted list says how many entries matched and gives the
reason for each that did not.

## Choose a question

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

The question menu first states what Loupe takes from the selection: the people, the dates, the places and the kinds of
item. It names any chosen items that are not in this case and leaves them out.

It then offers suggested questions, each with its count. The first is chosen for you. The chosen question appears as
fields you can change: **When**, **Nights**, **Who**, **What** and **Compare with**. Loupe picks the ordinary instances
to compare with and says how many; you can change them. **Loupe will check** lists the checks the question
runs. Press **Corroborate** to run it. Nothing runs before you press it.

## Narrow the question with the scope bar

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

The **Scope** section limits what the question is asked of. It never removes anything from the selection.

- **From** and **To** limit the dates. **Time zone** names the zone the hours are read in.
- **Repeats** sets a recurring window, such as every Friday from 22 to 4. Choose **Not recurring** to remove it.
- **Around each payment** sets a window of minutes before and after each payment, to anyone or to one person. The line
  below says how many payments have a time, and how many have only a date and so cannot take a window of minutes.
- **Record kinds** keeps only the kinds you tick, each with its count.
- **People** keeps the names you type, separated by commas.
- **Round an incident** lists the dates your theories and findings state, each with the words it was read from. **± 1
  hour**, **± 1 day** and **± 1 week** set the scope round that date. The hour button is off for a date with no time.

The heading counts the filters and the items in scope. Each filter shows as a chip; **Clear the scope** removes them
all. **Outside the scope** counts what each filter leaves out, by kind. Items with no time stay in, and the line says
how many.

## Group the selection

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

**Group these** splits the selection by **Person**, **Day**, **Place**, **Amount** or **App**; **Not grouped** undoes
it. Each group shows its count.

- **Ask about this group** runs the question on that group against the others. A group that names no person has no one
  to compare with, so the button is off.
- **Each group against the others** runs every group in turn and lists a link to each result.

The panel says how many items sit in more than one group, how many fit no group and why, and how many groups are not
listed or not run.

## Read the level

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

The result opens with the question, a level and one sentence with counts on both sides, such as "5 of 5 incident
Fridays, none of the 11 other Fridays". A stated rule sets the level from the signals on the page. No model sets it.

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| Strong link | Three or more kinds of evidence set the incidents apart from ordinary days, the pattern repeats, and no other explanation is left open |
| Probable link | Two or more kinds set the incidents apart, or three do with one other explanation still open |
| Possible link | One kind sets the incidents apart, or two weak kinds do |
| Coincidence likely | What was found is as common on ordinary days, is explained another way, or only describes how the items were picked |
| Can't tell | Too little data, or too few ordinary days to compare with; the sentence names what is missing |

**No link found** is a search outcome, not a level. It means the records needed were there and nothing linked the
people on the instances checked. It always says what was searched and that this is not proof that no link exists.

## What was present on each instance

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

**What was present, and on which instances** compares each signal across the incidents asked about and the ordinary
instances. Each mark reads present, checked and absent, or no data. No data counts neither way. The last two columns
count each signal on the incidents and on ordinary days, so you can see which signals set the incidents apart.

Press a mark to list the records behind it. A signal may carry a note: **Weighed low** with the reason, **Shown, not
weighed**, or that it describes how the other instances were picked and is not evidence.

**What raises it, and what lowers it** lists the reasons behind the level.

## Checks made

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

**Checks made** lists the ordinary explanations Loupe tests for every result: routine contact, a shared household, a
shared workplace, a stock phrase, a busy place, the clock or time zone, a recycled number, another person of the same
name, a shared device, duplicate copies and received media. Each reads **Ruled out**, **Weighed low**, **Checked,
nothing found** or **No data to check**, with its reason and the records behind it. A check with no data is a gap to
fill before relying on the level.

## Gaps and next questions

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

**Not in this pattern** names people with no link to the pattern, each with what was searched and what was out of
reach. **Records missing** lists gaps in the records for these instances. A gap can hide a link, so a Can't tell or a
weak level with gaps listed may change when the missing records arrive.

**Look further** offers next questions on the same items, each with its count. Pressing one runs it and opens its
result. **Ask the assistant to explain** opens the case assistant on the result.

## Read one instance person by person

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

Press an instance heading in the comparison to open **What happened, person by person**. It lists, for each person, the
records the comparison cites for that instance, numbered in the order they happened, each with its time and kind.
Copies of the same record show once, with the number of copies. Use it to read the sequence of one night or one
meeting. **Close** returns to the result.

## Trail of a look built in steps

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

When a look was built in more than one step, **Trail: how this was built** lists each step: what it added, the level
after it, and whether the level went up, went down or did not change. A step whose items changed nothing reads **added
nothing**. **Open this step without the later ones** shows the result as it stood at that step.

## Saved subsets

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

**Saved subsets** keeps the selection under a name so you can bring it back later. To combine two saved subsets,
choose a first and a second and how to combine them: in both, in either, in the first and not the second, or in the
second and not the first. **Combine into the selection** adds the result and states the count of each side. Choose only
a first subset to add it back as it is.

## Corroborate this claim

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

It tests a sentence someone said about where they were, who they were with or whom they contacted against the phones.
Open it from **Check a claim** in the tray, or from **Corroborate this claim** beside a theory line's reason or a
line of a statement check on the Patterns page. Those two open with the line already filled in.

1. Type or check the sentence. Add the **Date of the statement** if the sentence says "yesterday" or "last night".
2. Press **Read the sentence**. Loupe reads it into **Who**, **When**, **Where** and **What**, each with the words it
   came from and what read it. These are readings, not confirmed.
3. Set **Who said it**, and correct each field. A place needs a position and a radius. Without one, a claim about where
   someone was reads Can't tell.
4. Press **Confirm and corroborate**. Nothing is weighed before you confirm, and the confirmed claim is saved under your
   name with the sentence as written.

The result quotes the sentence and gives one of three answers: **Fits**, **Contradicts** or **Can't tell**, with one
sentence. A stated rule sets it from the phones' records, not a model. It names who confirmed the claim and which
fields they changed from Loupe's reading.

**What the records show** gives the reasons and the records behind them, each with its time and whether it was inside
or outside the place. **What was searched** names the window and counts the records read, by kind, and lists any gaps.
Can't tell means the records cannot settle the claim; the gaps say what would.

## Open a question the assistant drafted

This part appears only where the newer Corroborate has been turned on for you.

The case assistant can draft a Corroborate question over items it found and offer a link in its answer. The link adds
those items to your selection and opens the question menu with the drafted question chosen, marked as drafted by the
assistant. Nothing has run. Check the question, change it if needed, and press **Corroborate**.

## When a selection is too large

A look compares every pair of items, so the pairs grow fast. A group of more than 400 items is refused with the number
of pairs it would make. Narrow the selection, use the scope bar, or split it with **Group these**. Nothing is saved when
a look is refused.
